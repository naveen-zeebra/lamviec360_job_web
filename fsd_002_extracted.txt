1. Purpose & Scope
This Functional Specification Document elaborates the public-facing job portal and the authenticated Job Seeker Portal (JSP) into implementation-ready detail. The two are combined into a single document because they form one continuous journey: a guest browses jobs anonymously (Public Portal) and, on applying, is required to authenticate and continues into the Job Seeker Portal proper. Screen IDs p01–p05 are pre-login/public; js01, js03–js13 (js02 and js08 are reserved, unassigned IDs) are post-login.
This document should be read alongside:
LV360-FSD-001 — Authentication & Account Management (registration/login triggered from Apply)
LV360-FSD-004 — Candidate Pipeline & Team Management (employer side of the application lifecycle)
LV360-FSD-006 — Notifications & Communication (status-change and interview emails)

2.1 Gap Identified During BRD Alignment — Multiple Resume Support
BRD requirement LV-BRD-REQ-0003 explicitly calls for 'ongoing management of professional resumes' (plural), and this is reinforced directly by Business Rule LV-BR-0007: 'A Talent may maintain multiple resumes tailored for different industries, roles, or career objectives.' The BRD goes further and flags this as a competitive differentiator — most competing platforms support only a single resume.
The original PRD-derived JSP-FR-004 only specified a single resume/document upload as part of the profile. This is a genuine capability gap, not a documentation gap — the underlying data model and screens need to change. This revision adds JSP-FR-004b (Section 6) to close it; see the Open Items section for the follow-on screen-design question this raises.

5. Functional Specifications — Public Portal
JSP-FR-001  — Public Job Search
The system shall provide public-facing job search, accessible without login, filterable by keyword, job title, location (province/city), industry, salary range (VND), and job type (full-time, part-time, remote, contract).
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: p01, p02, p05
JSP-FR-002  — Job Search Results
Search results shall be presented as job cards showing: Job Title, Company Name, Logo, Location, Salary Range (VND), Date Posted, and Job Type tag. Results shall be sortable by date and by relevance.
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: p01, p02
JSP-FR-003  — Job Detail Page
The Job Detail page shall display the full job description, qualifications, company description, application deadline, location, and salary range, with an 'Apply Now' action that requires login before the application can proceed.
Salary Range display format: "[Min] - [Max] VND (Gross/Net)". If Negotiable, display: "Mức lương: Thỏa thuận"
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: p03   |   Business Rules: BR-009
JSP-FR-013a  — Homepage Events Widget
The Homepage shall include a section showing events for the current month (seminars, webinars, exhibitions related to job seekers and employers) that candidates can click through to review externally hosted event details.
Priority: P2 Should Have   |   Screens: p01, js01
JSP-FR-013b  — Employer Directory Slider
The Homepage/landing page shall show a slide-style list of employer companies with their current job openings.
Priority: P2 Should Have   |   Screens: p01, p05
6. Functional Specifications — Job Seeker Portal
JSP-FR-004  — Job Seeker Profile
Job Seekers shall create and maintain a profile covering personal details, work experience, education, skills, languages, desired salary range (VND), and resume/document upload.
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: js07, js10
Field-Level Detail:
Accepted upload formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG
Maximum file size: 10MB. Example: Resume (3MB) + Portfolio (5MB) + Certificate (2MB) = 10MB total. If total exceeds 10MB: error message "Total file size cannot exceed 10MB. Please reduce file sizes or remove some documents."
JSP-FR-004b  — Multiple Resume Management (new — added per BRD LV-BR-0007)
A Job Seeker shall be able to maintain more than one named resume/CV within their profile — e.g. 'Software Engineer CV', 'Product Management CV' — each independently uploadable or built from profile data, editable, and deletable. One resume shall be marked as Default. This extends js07/js10 and is a required addition, not an optional enhancement, per BRD LV-BR-0007.
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: js07, js09, js10   |   Business Rules: LV-BR-0007
Field-Level Detail:
Resume list view (extends js09 Document Archive): Resume Name, Last Updated, Default flag, Used-in-Application count
'Set as Default' action — the Default resume is what One-Click Apply (JSP-FR-005) pre-fills unless the candidate explicitly picks a different one at the point of application
Application Detail (js05) should show which named resume was submitted with a given application, for the candidate's own reference
JSP-FR-005  — One-Click Apply
JSP-FR-005a
Job Seekers shall be able to apply using their saved profile in one click, with an optional cover letter. 
- Profile completeness must be ≥ 60% to enable Apply button (per BRD BR-101-02). 
- If < 60%: Apply button disabled with tooltip: "Please complete your profile to at least 60% before applying." 
- Completeness calculation: (filled required fields / total required fields) × 100% 
- Required fields: Name, Email, Phone, Location, Work Experience (≥1), Education (≥1), Skills (≥3), Resume (≥1)
The system shall pre-populate the application form from the profile, using the Default resume (JSP-FR-004b) unless the candidate selects a different named resume for this specific application. A mandatory candidate data consent checkbox shall be displayed before final submission; applications cannot be submitted without explicit consent.
JSP-FR-005b
Candidates who have been rejected for a job cannot reapply to the same job for 90 days (per BRD BR-301-09).
Field-Level Detail: 
- If candidate previously applied and was rejected: Apply button disabled 
- Tooltip message: "You applied for this position on [date] and were not selected. You can reapply after [date + 90 days]." 
- If job is reposted by employer (new Job ID): reapplication allowed immediately 
- System automatically unlocks reapplication after 90 days 
- Reapplication treated as new application (fresh pipeline, no history carried over)
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: js03   |   Business Rules: BR-011   |   Acceptance Criteria: AC-004, AC-016
JSP-FR-006  — Saved Jobs
Job Seekers shall be able to save listings to a personal 'Saved Jobs' list for later review.
- Maximum 100 saved jobs per user (per BRD BR-201-05). 
- Saved jobs automatically expire and are removed after 90 days if the job is closed (per BRD BR-201-04). 
- Expired saved jobs show "Job No Longer Available" status with option to remove from list. 
- User receives notification 7 days before expiry: "Your saved job '[Job Title]' will expire in 7 days."
Priority: P2 Should Have   |   Screens: js06
JSP-FR-007  — Application Tracker
The Application Tracker shall show all submitted applications with current status: Applied, Under Review, Shortlisted, Phone Screening, Interview Scheduled, Offer Sent, Hired, Rejected. The progress visualization should be a clear, modern stepper (an improvement over prior baseline design per UX feedback).
Job Seekers shall be able to withdraw their application before it reaches the Screening stage. 
Field-Level Detail: 
- "Withdraw Application" button visible only when status = "Applied" (before HR review) 
- Confirmation modal: "Are you sure you want to withdraw this application? This action cannot be undone." 
- Withdrawn applications move to "Withdrawn" status (not deleted, for audit trail) 
- Withdrawn applications excluded from "Active Applications" count 
- Employer notified of withdrawal (optional, configurable)
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: js04, js05   |   Acceptance Criteria: AC-005
JSP-FR-007b  — Estimated Stage Duration Indicator
The Application Tracker/Detail view may optionally display an estimated stage-duration indicator per stage, based on the hiring company's average processing time, sourced from that company's pipeline configuration.
Priority: P2 Should Have   |   Screens: js04, js05
JSP-FR-008  — Status Notifications
The system shall push notifications (in-app and email) to the Job Seeker on: application confirmed, CV viewed, status change, phone screening scheduled (Vietnam practice), interview invitation, offer received. 
Detailed message content and triggers are specified in LV360-FSD-006 — Notifications & Communication.
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: js04, js12   |   Acceptance Criteria: AC-008
JSP-FR-009  — AI Interview Preparation
A Job Seeker shall be able to select a saved application and receive AI-generated practice interview questions relevant to that job's description.
Priority: P2 Should Have   |   Screens: js05, js11   |   Acceptance Criteria: AC-019
JSP-FR-010  — Rule-Based Job Recommendations
- The system shall show rule-based job recommendations ('Similar Jobs') on the Job Seeker Dashboard and Job Detail page. 
- Recommendation algorithm (Phase 1 - Rule-Based): 
- Match by same category (40%) 
- Match by same location (30%)
- Match by overlapping skills (20%) 
- Match by salary range (10%) 
- Recommendations updated daily based on user activity (apply/save/browse). 
- Phase 2 will introduce ML-based collaborative filtering (user behavior patterns) and deep learning (semantic job matching).
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: p03, js01   |   Acceptance Criteria: AC-018
JSP-FR-011  — Guided Profile Completion
The system shall prompt the Job Seeker to complete any missing required profile fields immediately after login.
The system shall display a profile completeness progress bar (0-100%) on the Dashboard (js01) and Profile Editor (js07). Real-time updates as user fills required fields. Tooltip shows missing fields when hovering over incomplete sections
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: js01, js07
JSP-FR-012  — Candidate Document Archive
The system shall provide password-protected, encrypted storage for the Job Seeker's previous employment contracts, relieving letters, payslips, and certificates. Documents are uploaded by the Job Seeker and are accessible only to the Job Seeker and — from the Shortlisted stage onward — the employer, per the visibility rule in EMP-FR-026b (see LV360-FSD-004).
Document Archive storage limits: 
- Per-file limit: 10MB 
- Total storage per user: 100MB (across all archived documents) 
- Storage quota displayed in js09 with usage indicator 
- When quota exceeded: user must delete old documents before uploading new ones
Priority: P1 Must Have   |   Screens: js09, js10
7. Process Flows
Actor color key: teal = Job Seeker/Guest, navy = Employer/Company Admin/HR, plum = Super Admin, orange = AI, slate = System (automated step, no human actor).
7.1 Guest Job Search to Apply (Login-Gated)
Covers JSP-FR-001–003. Trigger: an anonymous visitor lands on the Homepage. Outcome: the visitor authenticates and continues into Apply, or leaves without applying. Hands off to LV360-FSD-001, Flow 1/2 for login/registration.
7.2 One-Click Apply (Multi-Resume & Consent)
Covers JSP-FR-005 and JSP-FR-004b. Trigger: an authenticated Job Seeker reaches the Apply Form. Outcome: a submitted application, or a blocked submission if the mandatory consent checkbox (BR-011) is unchecked.
7.3 Application Tracking & Notification
Covers JSP-FR-007, JSP-FR-007b, JSP-FR-008. Trigger: HR moves a candidate to a new pipeline stage (LV360-FSD-004, Flow 1). Outcome: the candidate is notified (unless HR suppressed it per NTF-FR-006) and sees the updated status.
7.4 AI Interview Preparation
Covers JSP-FR-009. Trigger: a candidate at the Interview Scheduled stage opens AI Interview Prep. Outcome: AI-generated practice questions relevant to the job description.

12. Open Items & Clarifications Needed
The PRD assigns the ID JSP-FR-013 to two distinct requirements — the Homepage events widget and the employer directory slider. This document has renumbered them JSP-FR-013a and JSP-FR-013b respectively for traceability; recommend the PRD be corrected to match before this FSD is baselined.
Screen IDs js02 and js08 are reserved but unassigned in the PRD's screen inventory. No functionality is missing as a result, but confirm with the UX team that no screen was dropped during design.
OTP/resend cooldown and reset-link expiry are governed by LV360-FSD-001 (AUTH); this document assumes the Apply flow (js03) hands off to that spec whenever an unauthenticated user triggers login from p03.
New from BRD review — JSP-FR-004b (multiple resumes) reuses screen js09, previously scoped only as a 'Document Archive' for past-employment paperwork (contracts, payslips). Recommend UX confirm whether resume management and employment-document storage should be two visually distinct tabs within js09, or whether js09 should be split into two screens — the underlying purpose (named CVs vs. verification documents) is different enough that combining them may confuse candidates.
The Resume-Name and Default-flag data model implied by JSP-FR-004b is not present anywhere in the original PRD's data model (Section 5) or screen inventory. This is a genuine scope addition, not a documentation fix — flagging for Solution Architect sizing before this is committed to a sprint.
